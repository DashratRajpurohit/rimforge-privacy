Rimforge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Rimforge is an offline arcade game. This policy describes the Android version of Rimforge.

Game progress

The game saves your stage, equipment, coins, and settings in storage on your device. This information is used to resume your game. It is not sent to the developer. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. The Android app does not enable cloud backup.

Data collection and sharing

The Android app does not request Internet access, require an account, show ads, or include analytics. It does not intentionally collect or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Children

Rimforge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
